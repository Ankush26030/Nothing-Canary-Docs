# Privacy Policy for NOTHING Canary NOTHING Canary is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, utility, music, welcome, and other Discord-related features. We respect your privacy and aim to collect and retain only the information necessary to operate and improve the Bot. ## 1. Information We Collect Depending on the features used, NOTHING Canary may process or store the following information: **Discord Server IDs** — used to identify servers where the Bot is installed and to provide server-specific features. **Discord User IDs** — used for user-specific features, moderation actions, permissions, and related functionality. **Server Configuration Data** — settings required to configure and operate Bot features, including welcome, moderation, music, and utility settings. **Command and Feature Data** — limited information related to Bot commands or feature usage when necessary for functionality, troubleshooting, security, or moderation. **Member-Related Data** — information associated with Discord server members, such as member join or leave events, when required for features such as welcome messages, moderation, or member management. NOTHING Canary does not intentionally store the full content of users' Discord messages unless a specific feature requires it and the Bot clearly states that such data is being collected or stored. ## 2. How We Use Information We use collected information only as necessary to: * Provide and operate NOTHING Canary's features. * Save and apply server-specific settings. * Detect member-related events for features such as welcome and moderation systems. * Process message content when required for specific Bot features. * Perform moderation, utility, music, and other requested actions. * Maintain security and prevent abuse. * Diagnose technical issues and improve the Bot. ## 3. Server Members Intent NOTHING Canary may use Discord's **Server Members Intent** to receive member-related events and information required for features such as welcoming new members, detecting member joins or leaves, and performing moderation or member-management functionality. The Server Members Intent is used only when necessary for these Bot features and is not intended to collect unnecessary personal information. ## 4. Message Content When NOTHING Canary has access to Discord's **Message Content Intent**, it may process the content of Discord messages in real time when required for features such as commands, moderation, automations, or other functionality that depends on message content. Unless explicitly stated otherwise for a particular feature, message content is not permanently stored by NOTHING Canary. Message content is processed only as necessary for the relevant Bot functionality, security, moderation, troubleshooting, or abuse prevention. Users currently do not have a separate option to opt out of Message Content processing when Message Content access is required for a specific Bot feature to function. ## 5. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or trade user information. Data may only be disclosed when reasonably necessary to: * Comply with applicable laws or legal requirements. * Protect the security and integrity of NOTHING Canary. * Investigate abuse, fraud, or other misuse of the Bot. ## 6. Data Storage and Security Any information stored by NOTHING Canary is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the Bot's functionality. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. ## 7. Data Deletion You may request deletion of data associated with your Discord server or user ID by contacting the NOTHING Canary development team through the official support server or the contact method provided below. Removing NOTHING Canary from a Discord server may stop future data collection for that server, but does not automatically guarantee deletion of previously stored data unless the Bot's implementation is designed to delete it automatically. ## 8. Your Choices Users may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the NOTHING Canary development team. There is currently no separate opt-out mechanism for Message Content processing when such processing is required for a Bot feature to function. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to NOTHING Canary, its features, or applicable requirements. ## 10.
